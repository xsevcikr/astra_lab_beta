## Method

* Published side: each number has a 1-sigma error bar and a role (`data/validation-uncertainty.js`).
* Simulator side: the nominal flight, plus the spread of the same number over the dispersed flights. Dispersions (1 sigma):
  * Engine thrust (each engine group): 1.5 %. Acceptance-test spread of liquid engines and solid motors is about 1 to 2 %.
  * Specific impulse (each engine group): 0.5 %. Isp is repeatable to about 0.5 % on flight engines.
  * Propellant loaded (each stage): 0.5 %. Loading accuracy and residuals, about 0.5 %.
  * Dry mass of each part: 2.0 %. Published dry masses are nominal; real vehicles differ by a few per cent.
  * Air density (whole flight): 5.0 %. Day-to-day change of the atmosphere, about 5 %; also stands in for the drag coefficient, which is not measured for these models.
* z = (simulated − published) / sqrt(σ_sim² + σ_pub²). Model-form error = max(0, |d| − 2 σ_c).
* Roles:
  * `prediction`: The simulator worked it out from the physics. **Counts as evidence.**
  * `consistency`: Follows almost directly from published inputs (e.g. propellant / mass flow).
  * `calibrated`: Something in the model was tuned until this matched. Not evidence.
  * `target`: The guidance steers to this value.
  * `plan`: The mission plan sets this time; the physics only fills in the gaps.
  * `build`: Checks the vehicle was entered correctly.
  * `verification`: Textbook formula against a textbook number: tests the code.
