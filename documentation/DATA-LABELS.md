# Data labels

Every component, body and data value in ASTRA LAB carries one of four labels, shown as a badge in the Vehicle Designer and listed in reports:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Real** | Based on published data of a real product or place (for example the radius and GM of Earth, the U.S. Standard Atmosphere 1976, the Cape Canaveral launch site coordinates). |
| **Estimated** | An engineering estimate that is not an official specification (for example thermal limits of generic materials, drag tables). |
| **User-created** | Created or edited by you. Editing a built-in component turns it into a user-created one. |
| **Fictional** | A generic, invented component. **All 46 built-in catalogue components are fictional generic parts**, not real products of any manufacturer. |

Why: results are only as good as the inputs. A rocket built from fictional engines can teach and compare designs but says nothing about a real vehicle. To study a real vehicle, create components with data you trust and mark them accordingly.

Settings → *Data labels* switches the badges on or off. Reports always list the labels present in the vehicle.
