# ASTRA LAB 2.2: changelog (from 2.1)
- **New look:** deep night-sky theme with a slowly drifting starfield behind every screen (turn off with `localStorage.setItem('astra.sky','0')`), glass panels, cyan accents, heavy wide headlines and pill buttons.
- **Home:** an "Explore Beyond" hero with Earth from orbit (NASA imagery), floating rocks and big pill buttons. All buttons and vehicle cards are unchanged.
- **Easier to find things:** the menu is grouped (Start, Design, Fly, Understand, Study, Project) and every expert name shows its plain meaning underneath, for example "Engineering Analysis / What happened?". No screen or number was removed.
- Version 2.2.0. All unit tests (322) and every browser suite pass, including the 600-layout check.
