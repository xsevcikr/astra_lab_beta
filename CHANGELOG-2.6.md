# ASTRA LAB 2.6: look, motion and cockpit

Everything here is presentation only. No result, flight or validation number changed.

## 1. Mission-control cockpit
- Glass instrument panel over the 3D view in **Mission Control** and **Replay**: speed dial, height dial, artificial horizon (pitch), big **T+ clock**, Mach, dynamic pressure, push (g), throttle, fuel bar, and a **ribbon of flight events** (blue ticks, red for problems, white marker = now, last event named above it).
- Button **Cockpit** (left, under the camera buttons) switches it on or off. It replaces the plain number box while it is on; the side panel and charts still carry every number. Remembered on this computer.
- Code: `ui/lib/cockpit.js`, wired in `ui/lib/flightdraw.js`, `ui/screens/control.js`, `ui/screens/replay.js`.

## 2. Rocket look
- Painted stages get a **colour band** under the top dome and, depending on the part, a **black roll pattern** or **checker pattern** (picked from the part's seed, so a rocket always looks the same).
- A tight **clear-coat highlight** on painted skin (second specular lobe).
- A thin bright **vapour ring** around the rocket for the moment it crosses Mach 1 in humid low air (on top of the existing condensation cloud).
- The existing exhaust glow that lights the rocket and the ground is unchanged.

## 3. Space scenes
- **City glow**: night lights now bloom softly instead of being single points.
- **Aurora** ovals around both poles on the night side (green with purple curtain peaks), strongest at the planet's edge.
- **Sunlight glitter** on the sea (second broad highlight with a fine wave sparkle).
- Moon and Mars: no better maps could be downloaded in this environment, so they are unchanged.

## 4. Motion and sound
- Screens glide in; panels rise; **parallax stars** behind the menus follow the mouse; whole numbers in the stat boxes **count up**.
- **T-minus 3-2-1-LIFTOFF** over the 3D view when you press Start in Mission Control. The flight is held for those 3 seconds. Skipped when animations are off or when an automated browser runs the app.
- With **Play small sound effects** on: tick for each countdown second, a launch chime, and a low **engine rumble** that follows the throttle and fades with height. The same single switch turns all of it off.
- Settings → **Look and motion**: animation switch. Reduced-motion in Windows turns animations off automatically.

## 5. Hologram builder
- In the Vehicle Designer the 3D view turns into a blue **hologram** (grid, scan lines, sweeping beam, label) for about a second after every change, then the real model fades back in.

## 6. Design system
- Shared glass panels with glow edges on hover, a consistent glow for focus and hover, pill badges, gradient progress bars, consistent type scale tokens (`--fs-*`, `--sp-*`).
- Settings → **Colour look**:
  - **Deep space** (dark, default)
  - **Daylight** (light, for bright rooms; the 3D views stay dark on purpose). The light twins of the dark rules are generated by `scripts/gen-daylight.py` from `ui/styles.css`.
  - **High contrast** (black and white outlines, yellow focus ring)

## Tests
- New `tests/ui-v26.mjs` (14 checks): star field, looks, animation switch, Settings controls, T-minus intro holds the flight, cockpit elements and clock, cockpit switch, hologram layer, no page or shader errors.

## Honest limits
- Livery has no lettering or logos, only bands and patterns.
- Aurora is static (no flicker) and is drawn in the Earth shader only; it is hard to see in daylight views, by design.
- Daylight look is generated from the dark rules; a few rarely used screens may still show dark patches.
- Sounds are synthesised (noise and tones), not recordings.
- The 3D picture in this environment was checked with a software renderer; frame rates on a real GPU are expected to be higher, not lower.
